The observed CPOSS single-component size and mass ranges lie within OMC25’s ranges. Range overlap does not establish matching chemical environments or crystal packings.

The other 44 verified CPOSS systems are complete multicomponent formula units. They retain coformers, counterions and water; their masses are not single-molecule masses. One additional single-component system remains in the formula-only sensitivity population.

Molar masses use one frozen average-atomic-weight convention. Original OMC25 masses are retained; harmonization changes a training mass by at most 0.032 g mol⁻¹.

Density coverage and weighting

114,284available source density records
187single-component systems

113,265 records from 258 searches lie in the comparison window. 0.89% lie outside it and remain in the data tables.

Each search contributes one archive-linked density column. The CPOSS curves give each molecular system equal total weight within the shared window, with equal weights for its represented searches. This prevents systems with more stored candidates from dominating. OMC25 and CSD are independently normalized histogram references; their molecular-system identities are unavailable for reweighting.

This visualization subset is not a density census of all 517,594 archived files. Density agreement does not establish equal chemical, packing or stability coverage.
