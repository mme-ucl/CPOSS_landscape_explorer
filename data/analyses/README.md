# CPOSS figures and statistics

Source snapshot: 2026-10-27. Revision: 6a77cc47b35f268f81a1d433b62c3e0917e20ffd.

The page reports 310 current JSON records across 234 molecular systems, 517,594 model-specific archived RES files, and 508 model archives. It does not claim geometric uniqueness. The analysis and Navigator now use the same JSON inventory.

The corrected chemical comparison contains 189 coordinate-verified single-component systems, 44 verified complete multicomponent formula units, and a separate 190-system metadata-only sensitivity population. OMC25 uses 44,403 training molecular identifiers.

Density statistics use 114,284 archive-linked CPOSS visualization records from 187 systems. The OMC25 and organic CSD references are approximate recoveries from paper Figure 3a. Comparisons are conditional on 0.8–2.4 g cm⁻³, with common 0.02-wide bins. Atomic fractions and molecular elemental prevalence are separate measurements.

The molecular map contains 307 of 310 records. Its multicomponent representatives are the largest non-water components, not complete formula units.

Files: summary.json contains aggregate statistics and the five-figure catalogue; build-manifest.json records source checksums. The five CSVs are the complete result tables linked by the retained figures. The ZIP includes only these figures, captions and linked tables. Earlier overview exports, sketch-map diagnostics and separate molecular illustrations are excluded from the public downloads. Original analysis files and previously exported CSV copies remain on disk, but are not linked by this page or included in its download catalogue or ZIP. Source workbooks and large source caches are not included.

Sources: https://louiseprice.github.io/CPOSS_Database/index.html ; https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/OMC25 ; https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-06628-2 .
