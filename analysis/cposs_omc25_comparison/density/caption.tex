\caption{\textbf{Crystal-density distributions in CPOSS, OMC25 and the CSD.} \textbf{(A)} Probability densities in common 0.02\,g\,cm$^{-3}$ bins. \textbf{(B)} Cumulative probabilities. Both panels show the same system-balanced CPOSS population and the OMC25 last-relaxation-frame and organic CSD 6.00 references recovered from Fig.~3a of the OMC25 paper\cite{gharakhanyan2026omc25}. The available CPOSS sample comprises 114,284 archive-linked source-reported density records from 258 searches and 187 coordinate-validated single-component molecular systems, rather than a complete census of archived structures. Each search contributes one uniquely matched density column from its lowest-numbered eligible archived energy model. Curves are conditional on 0.8--2.4\,g\,cm$^{-3}$; 0.89\% of available CPOSS records fall outside this interval. Within the interval, CPOSS gives equal total weight to each molecular system, equal weights to its searches and equal weights to records within a search. Reference histograms are conservatively rebinned by bin overlap and independently normalised by their recovered count mass; molecular-system identities are unavailable for reference reweighting. All probability densities have unit area and all cumulative distributions end at one. The CPOSS cumulative curve uses source values; reference cumulative curves assume uniform probability within rebinned bins and remain approximate. No raw-count or structure-weighted CPOSS curve is shown.}
