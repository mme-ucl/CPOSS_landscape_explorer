\subsection{Chemical composition and molecular-size coverage}
We compared the chemical content of CPOSS with the training split of Open Molecular Crystals 2025 (OMC25)\cite{gharakhanyan2026omc25}, counting each documented CPOSS molecular system and each OMC25 CSD reference code once (Fig.~\ref{fig:cposs_omc25_comparison}). The principal comparison contains 189 single-component CPOSS systems with coordinate-validated compositions and 44,403 OMC25 molecular records. Repeated CSP searches, energy models, generated packings and relaxation frames do not weight these molecular distributions. A further 44 CPOSS multicomponent systems are described separately on their complete stoichiometric formula-unit basis. One CPOSS system with no valid alternative coordinate element table was excluded from the coordinate-validated population; including the reported formula gives a supplementary metadata-only population of 190 single-component systems.

CPOSS single-component systems contain 5--100 atoms per molecule (median 18), compared with 4--164 atoms (median 39) in OMC25. The corresponding median non-hydrogen atom counts are 12 and 21. Using one average-atomic-weight convention for both datasets, CPOSS molar masses span 32.04--890.88\,g\,mol$^{-1}$ (median 180.16\,g\,mol$^{-1}$), whereas OMC25 spans 30.03--1386.32\,g\,mol$^{-1}$ (median 312.15\,g\,mol$^{-1}$). These summaries describe the observed molecular populations and do not establish equivalence of their chemical environments or crystal packings.

Elemental occurrence is expressed as the fraction of molecular records containing each species. Oxygen occurs in 78.8\% of CPOSS systems and 81.9\% of OMC25 molecular records, while nitrogen occurs in 71.4\% and 65.4\%, respectively. The verified CPOSS population also contains Cu, outside the elemental vocabulary of OMC25. Its occurrence does not imply that OMC25 models have been evaluated on this chemistry. CPOSS additionally includes salts, solvates and co-crystals, which require a distinct formula-unit basis and are outside OMC25's stated single-component scope. The comparison therefore describes complementary sampling priorities: repeated crystal-energy-landscape searches in CPOSS and broad molecular sampling for training interatomic potentials in OMC25. The supplied reference figure provides no CSD elemental, molecular-size or mass distributions, so no such CSD curves are inferred here.

\subsection{Crystal-density distributions}
We compared CPOSS crystal densities with the OMC25 last-frame and organic CSD 6.00 reference histograms in Fig.~3a of the OMC25 paper\cite{gharakhanyan2026omc25} (Fig.~\ref{fig:cposs_omc25_csd_density}). The CPOSS density population comprises 114,284 archive-linked visualisation records from 258 searches covering 187 single-component molecular systems. For each search, we selected the lowest-numbered archived energy model with an unambiguous density column labelled in g\,cm$^{-3}$, retained finite positive values whose filenames match that archive, and counted repeated filenames once per search. These are source-reported densities from the visualised subset, rather than a complete density census of all archived CPOSS files. Experimental-match annotations were retained because they also label predicted structures; the records are not claimed to be geometrically unique minima.

The OMC25 and CSD bar heights were recovered from the vector graphic and provide approximate binned references. Their different original bin widths were accounted for by conservative overlap rebinning to 0.02\,g\,cm$^{-3}$ bins. The comparison is conditional on 0.8--2.4\,g\,cm$^{-3}$; 0.89\% of the available CPOSS records fall outside this interval. Within the interval, each CPOSS molecular system has equal total weight, each of its represented searches has equal weight within that system, and each retained structure has equal weight within its search. This avoids domination by systems with more stored structures. The resulting CPOSS median is 1.39\,g\,cm$^{-3}$, compared with approximately 1.29\,g\,cm$^{-3}$ for OMC25 and 1.34\,g\,cm$^{-3}$ for CSD. Every displayed probability density integrates to one and every cumulative distribution reaches one. Molecular-system reweighting of OMC25 and CSD is unavailable because their recovered histograms contain no system identities; each reference is normalised independently by its recovered count mass in the shared interval. Record-weighted CPOSS statistics remain in the tables but are not plotted. Density agreement is descriptive and does not establish agreement in composition, packing or stability.
