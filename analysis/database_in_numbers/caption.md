The database in numbers. Metadata were extracted from 235 molecule pages reachable from the CPOSS index, representing 311 distinct CSP searches in repository snapshot d65c1732ab6db46b8af34e0646a760c56a8d559c (accessed 2026-10-23). Each explicitly linked search is one dataset; its energy models are not separate datasets. A, the 12 most frequently credited scientists, with full credit for each named contributor and no inferred authorship from paper author lists (286/311 searches have a named scientist). B, linked Chemiscope structure-array entries per dataset, ranked on a logarithmic axis (307/311 files; median 438); experimental-reference entries and repeated filename labels are retained, so 155,824 is a stored-entry total, not a count of globally distinct predictions or all structures originally generated. C, dataset frequencies for reported potential families and the six most frequent electronic-method categories; categories overlap and electronic levels may describe molecular or multipole calculations rather than periodic DFT. All method categories are tabulated. D, molar mass versus total atom count on logarithmic axes calculated from reported formulas, with one point per documented molecular system (235 systems). Multicomponent formulas retain explicitly written stoichiometry; hydrogens are included, and unit-cell atom counts are not used. E, annual CSP-search counts (bars) and stored structure entries (line), using single unambiguous years from the source Date field (236/311 datasets); date ranges and pre-2010 dates are excluded from annual bins. These are study dates, not release/deposition dates. F, the number of linked CSP papers per dataset and cumulative unique linked CSP papers by publication year (75 papers). Papers are deduplicated across datasets by DOI or canonical URL; explicit within-page Study_ID publication pointers are followed and labelled inferred. Experimental CSD citations are recorded separately. Helvetica is used and embedded throughout; the figure is A4 portrait, with a vector PDF and 300 dpi PNG.
