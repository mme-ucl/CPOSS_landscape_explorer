The CPOSS database in numbers. Metadata were extracted from 235 molecule pages reachable from the CPOSS index, representing 311 distinct CSP searches in repository snapshot d65c1732ab6db46b8af34e0646a760c56a8d559c (accessed 2026-10-23). Each explicitly linked search is one dataset; its energy models are not separate datasets. The headline counts 519,167 RES coordinate files in all 509 documented energy-model ZIP archives, verified against repository blob hashes. Separate representations under different models and any experimental references are retained; this is not a count of geometrically unique crystals or all candidates originally generated. Ancillary pDFT supplements and MacOS resource forks are excluded. All 311 datasets have complete archive counts. A, molar mass versus total atom count on logarithmic axes, calculated from reported formulas with one point per documented molecular system (235 systems). Multicomponent formulas retain written stoichiometry and hydrogens; unit-cell atom counts are not used. B, archived structure-file totals per dataset, ranked on a logarithmic axis (median 498). C, electronic structure theory level, showing dataset frequencies for the six most frequent categories. Categories overlap and may describe molecular or multipole calculations rather than periodic DFT. D, dataset credits for the 20 most frequently credited scientists, including Sally Price, with full credit for each explicitly named contributor and no authorship inferred from papers (286/311 searches have named credits; all 45 scientists remain in the tables). E, annual CSP-search counts (upper bars) and archived structure files (upper line), plus cumulative dataset counts (lower curve), using single unambiguous years from the source Date field (236/311 datasets; 488,411 structure files). The 75 searches with ranges or pre-2010 dates, comprising 30,756 files, are excluded from year bins; the cumulative curve ends at 236. Study dates do not measure release/deposition or structure-generation dates. F, cumulative unique linked CSP papers by publication year (75 papers), deduplicated by DOI or canonical URL; explicit within-page Study_ID publication pointers are followed and labelled inferred. Experimental CSD citations are recorded separately. The truncated Plasma colour ramp encodes within-panel logarithmic counts in B-D and a shared year range in E-F; A uses two fixed component colours and the upper E line is charcoal. Helvetica is embedded throughout; the figure is A4 portrait, with a vector PDF and 300 dpi PNG. Workbook and coordinate-archive counts disagree for 55 models; full comparisons and limitations are provided in structure_count_reconciliation.md.
