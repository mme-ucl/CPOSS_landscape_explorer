The CPOSS database in numbers. Metadata were extracted from 235 molecule pages reachable from the CPOSS index, representing 311 distinct CSP searches in repository snapshot d65c1732ab6db46b8af34e0646a760c56a8d559c (accessed 2026-10-23). Each explicitly linked search is one dataset; its energy models are not separate datasets. A, molar mass versus total atom count on logarithmic axes, calculated from reported formulas with one point per documented molecular system (235 systems). Multicomponent formulas retain explicitly written stoichiometry; hydrogens are included, and unit-cell atom counts are not used. B, linked Chemiscope structure-array entries per dataset, ranked on a logarithmic axis (307/311 files; median 438); experimental-reference entries and repeated filename labels are retained, so 155,824 is a stored-entry total, not a count of globally distinct predictions or all structures originally generated. Four linked JSON files are missing. C, electronic structure theory level, showing dataset frequencies for the six most frequent categories. Categories overlap and electronic levels may describe molecular or multipole calculations rather than periodic DFT. Complete potential-family and electronic-method data are tabulated separately. D, dataset credits for the 20 most frequently credited scientists, including Sally Price, with full credit for each named contributor and no inferred authorship from paper author lists (286/311 searches have a named scientist). The full metadata retain all 45 scientists. E, annual CSP-search counts (upper bars) and stored structure entries (upper line), plus cumulative CSP-dataset counts (lower curve), using single unambiguous years from the source Date field (236/311 datasets). Date ranges and pre-2010 dates are excluded from annual and cumulative bins; the cumulative curve therefore ends at 236, not 311. These are study dates, not release/deposition dates. F, cumulative unique linked CSP papers by publication year (75 papers). Papers are deduplicated across datasets by DOI or canonical URL; explicit within-page Study_ID publication pointers are followed and labelled inferred. Experimental CSD citations are recorded separately. Helvetica is used and embedded throughout; the figure is A4 portrait, with a vector PDF and 300 dpi PNG.
